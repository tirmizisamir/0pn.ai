# Code of Conduct

This project is a small, friendly space. Everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else connected to the project — is expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith and be patient with newcomers and non-native speakers.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, or publishing others' private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone who repeatedly ignores it. Decisions are made at the maintainers' discretion.

To report a problem, email the maintainer (see SECURITY.md) or use GitHub's "Report content" feature. Reports are handled privately.
